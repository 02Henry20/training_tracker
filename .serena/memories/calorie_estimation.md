Strength energy automatically estimates bout duration from completed reps at catalog tempo or hold seconds, plus catalog recovery bounded to 30–180 seconds (90 fallback). Entry restSeconds, repSeconds and durationMin are legacy fields ignored by calculations and no longer exposed in the editor. Workout duration is history only and adds no calories for idle time. Activity duration remains required for cardio. Category MET includes normal recovery; do not add recovery energy again. Historical calorie/XP totals recalculate; raw legacy fields are preserved.
